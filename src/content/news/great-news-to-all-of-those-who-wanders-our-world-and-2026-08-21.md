**Void Tales** is now available on Minecraft **26.2**.

We updated the server from 1.21.4 yesterday evening. The downtime lasted around 35 minutes; nobody was online at the time.

You don’t need to do anything. Your current instance will continue to work; the server will handle the update for you.

Optional, **but highly recommended**: The new “Void Tales 2.1.0” modpack is available on Modrinth and has been created for version 26.2. Import it as a new instance, your old one will remain untouched as a fallback.

**Now select your graphics options** (new modpack version)
- The first time you launch the game, the package will ask you once whether you want to enable ‘Full Visuals’. Select ‘Yes, enable’ or ‘Not now’
- You can change your choice at any time using the button on the title screen: ‘Enable Full Visuals’ / ‘Switch to Lite Visuals’

**No longer moving and switching annoying Layer 1 and Layer 2 ressourcepacks with crashes!**

- The game launches with the Core Pack, so you’ll reach the menu faster and will always get the ‘Void Tales’ look
- ‘Void Tales’ gear retains its custom models even in ‘Lite Visuals’ mode. Previously, these were only available with the full graphics tier.

New in this update
- The custom sky is back. Starry sky, auroras, shooting stars, sunrise and sunset, end-world sky. It had been silently disabled since version 2.0.5
- Blocks, armour and weapons have been redrawn based on the updated ‘Conquest’ model
- 22 shields feature themed designs instead of the standard design
- Clearer menus, and the title screen no longer breaks up into small windows
- Smaller download size: 127 MB instead of 186 MB

New on the server
- The map is now in 3D. Same link: dynmap.voidtales.win

Fixed
- Some quest steps had silently stopped working since the 10 August update. They are working again

Behind the scenes, there’s a lot of rewriting that you’re not actually meant to notice: models, the HUD, quests and cutscenes had to be completely overhauled for version 26.2 to ensure they behave exactly as they did before. If you notice any of these changes, it’s a bug - please report it to us.
