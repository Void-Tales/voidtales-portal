While it seems very quiet for you here, I am sitting in the shadows of my own world, tinkering, fiddling, and working on everything I can get my hands on.

Maybe you are slowly getting tired of this eternal “Void Tales is being worked on and will be finished at some point” thing, but what I am doing right now surpasses everything.

Some of you know that I never leave my own world, so I never visit other servers and never have, but I have a feeling that this is going to be special. I imagine that my creative way of doing things is generally unique, but now I finally have the opportunity to LET EVERYTHING RUN FREE! 

Whether you have to bribe an owl to fly you to the next island,

you can't shake the feeling that this statue is following you with its gaze,

you try to increase your meager remaining wealth and gamble with your life
(Void creatures love dice),

have to think carefully about what you say to this cat because it has a very good memory,

want to sit on this bench under the cherry blossom tree and suddenly have two raccoons keep you company to enjoy the view with you,

you can suddenly really play this flute with a real scale to play around with and reveal a secret with a certain melody,

you can travel into the NPC's memory to help her overcome her trauma,

or you have to decide the fate of a tiny spider because you want this gemstone.

Oh man, I'm telling you, that's not all.

VOID TALES WILL BE FULL OF THIS STUFF!

Why am I telling you this? Need to communicate! The nights of brilliant breakthroughs are lonely...
