Today we reveal something we are proud of.

The new official Void Tales logo.

This logo was created by the incredibly talented artist Romsesito (https://vgen.co/romsesito), who brought the identity of our world to life with care and artistic vision.

As promised:
AI is completely banned from the artistic side of Void Tales.

That philosophy matters deeply to us as a story-driven, atmospheric experience where immersion is everything.

Romsesito is also known for creating their work without the use of AI-generated art, which made this collaboration feel even more fitting for the direction of Void Tales.

And a special thank you to @snowflakekiwi for recommending this artist to us. That recommendation genuinely led to something special. Oh and to @cyvl of course who created the voidling originally exclusive for us.

We hope everyone love it as much as we do.
