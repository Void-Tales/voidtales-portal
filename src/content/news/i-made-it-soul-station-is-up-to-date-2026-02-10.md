The foundation for Soul Station has been laid. This place is now really fun for everyone.

Each NPC has unique interactions and ways of talking to you. The ticket machine is also new, and the Everwalker has a whole new function from now on. There are also new things like the /stuck command, for example.

But now it's really getting started... Soul Station only has 3 NPCs that you can make promises to (quests), and these still need to be expanded piece by piece with decisions and clues that you can collect along the way.

This place is now as it deserves to be, and what I'm writing here in this announcement can't even begin to describe how wonderful these little interactions are.

Show people the weapons you've collected, complain about prices, discuss philosophy, make promises, let yourself be shown around, collect books, and so on and so forth...

This is the beginning (again XD)

I notice how tired I am now and how much this announcement does no justice to the changes.
