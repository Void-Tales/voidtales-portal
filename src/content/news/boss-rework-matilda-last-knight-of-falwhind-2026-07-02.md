Matilda has received a complete gameplay and presentation overhaul.

While we always wanted her to be a difficult encounter for everyone, the fight often felt punishing in ways that were difficult to learn. Many players were defeated by mechanics they could not reasonably anticipate, making the encounter more frustrating than rewarding.

Our goal was not to make Matilda easier.

Our goal was to make her fairer, more readable, and more memorable, while strengthening her identity as one of the most important characters in Void Tales.

Changes:

- Renamed Matilda, Fatebound Guardian → Matilda, Last Knight of Falwhind
- Reworked her introduction, combat dialogue, and final words to create a more emotional and nostalgic encounter.
- Added subtle hints throughout the fight that slowly build toward her realization of who she is facing.
- Updated her soundtrack to better fit the atmosphere of the battle.
- Improved slash sound effects and visual effects for stronger combat feedback.
- Adjusted attack timers and RNG to create a more consistent and readable combat rhythm.
- Added a Parry icon above her head while she is in a counter stance.
- Added an Exhausted window after her charge attack, giving players a clear opportunity to retaliate.
- Reworked her parry counter. Instead of a nearly unavoidable one-shot, she now performs a sequence of her iconic sword slashes.
- Reduced the damage of several attacks while keeping the fight dangerous. A successful parry from Matilda is still capable of turning the battle around, but it is now less punishing for first-time players.
- Improved the overall pacing of the encounter to reduce inactive moments and make every phase feel more engaging.
- The boss's announcement title (name on the screen) has been upgraded.

Matilda should now feel less like an unfair obstacle and more like a duel against a legendary knight whose story slowly reveals as the battle progresses.
