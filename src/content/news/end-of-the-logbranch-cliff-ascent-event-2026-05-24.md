Hey everyone!

The rebuilt Logbranch Cliff has officially survived its first real test.

Over the last days, a few brave players climbed, failed, optimized routes, and pushed the area to its limits. And honestly, I learned a lot from this event.
This rework became something much bigger than just “fixing” an old area. It shaped the future of the entire parkour system in Void Tales.

The new Logbranch Cliff feels smooth, satisfying, and rewarding.
Soon nobody will even remember the old frustrating version anymore.

But now... the results.

She absolutely grinded this place and discovered what might be the fastest route possible.

🥈 Second Place — @14BK

Thank you. Seriously.

The climb may be over.
But the path ahead just became clearer.
