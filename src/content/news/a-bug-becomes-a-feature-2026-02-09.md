I recently had trouble with the tags resetting for you guys. This means, for example, that the camera movement showing the Soul Station will repeat itself after a certain amount of time.

I can easily change this by replacing the tag with a permission. No problem at all. And of course I will.

But then I realized that all interactions with the NPCs at Soul Station would also have to have these permissions... but wait, do they?

Don't get me wrong, interactions such as the camera movement or Amelia's robot friend Chromo zapping you with his laser should only happen once, but interactions with NPCs, i.e. the first dialogue, the “getting to know you” round, can happen again. Soul Station is caught in a kind of loop and calls all kinds of characters to itself in order to be a train station again. It wants to be what it was created for, and everyone there, except you, can't manage to leave.

So it would be fitting, right? They've been there for so long, somehow I like the idea that they've forgotten they ever met you.

And no, that won't apply to all NPCs, just Soul Station.
