Another big step in the Chapter 1 Quality Update is complete.

I am now roughly halfway through the entire Chapter 1 improvement process.

This update is not only about adding new content. It is about making everything and everyone that already exists feel better, smoother, and more memorable.

Nimue was already considered one of the better designed bosses, so this was not a complete redesign.

The goal was simple:

Make an already good boss even better.

---

** 🌸 Boss Fight Improvements**

Nimue's teleport mechanic received a major improvement.

Before, when she changed locations after being hit, players could get launched into the air. While interesting, it often felt like a punishment for successfully attacking her.

This has been changed.

Nimue's teleport now focuses on movement and atmosphere instead of interrupting the fight.

The combat flow should feel smoother and more rewarding.

---

**💧 Drift | A New Universal System**

Nimue's biggest change is the introduction of **Drift**.

Her projectiles no longer simply throw players upwards.

Instead, they apply the Drift status effect.

Drift causes the target to slowly float and lose contact with the ground.

This system is no longer exclusive to Nimue.

It is now a universal mechanic that can appear throughout the game.

The goal is to create mechanics that connect with each other instead of existing separately.

---

** 🏮 Nimue as a Companion | Formless Mass: Flimmer**

After defeating Nimue, she can now return as your own little vengeful wisp.

But this time, she is not hunting you.

She is fighting beside you.

Nimue will:

* Fire her familiar lantern projectiles
* Detect enemies affected by Drift
* React to Drift even if another ability caused it
* Create new combos with the player's own abilities

She does not simply repeat her boss mechanic.

She evolves it.

---

** ✨ Nimue's Light**

A new interaction was added:

If an enemy is affected by Drift, the player can call Nimue's attention to them.

She will prepare a special beam attack that:

* Always hits valid Drift targets
* Deals critical damage
* Has a short buildup before impact
* Comes with unique dialogue

Examples:

> "There you are..."

> "I found you..."

Nimue now understands the entire Drift system, not only her own abilities.

---

**🎁 New Nimue Boss Reward Chest**

Nimue's reward chest was also redesigned.

Boss rewards should feel like an experience, not just a loot drop.

The chest now has multiple tiers:

⭐ Tier 1 — Basic Nimue-themed rewards
⭐ Tier 2 — Special crates and resources
⭐ Tier 3 — Rare keys and unique rewards
⭐ Tier 4 — Valuable materials
⭐ Tier 5 — Sacred Relics

The highest tier can reward:

* Formless Mass Blueprint: **Flimmer**
* Nimue's exclusive mask
* Additional rewards after collecting previous relics

---

** 🎭 Nimue's Mask**

Players who complete Nimue's highest reward tier can now obtain her own mask.

A reminder that bosses in this world are more than just enemies.

They are stories.
