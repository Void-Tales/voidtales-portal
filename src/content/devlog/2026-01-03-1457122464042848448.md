The first thing I want to show you in the new content: Throwable Weapons.

They are not in your arsenal, but left somewhere from someone. A smooth way to expand the flow of combat and, of course, a nice change of pace.

And let's be honest: it just feels badass with the right sound design, which I have also found, by the way.

These weapons (currently only spears, but more will come) will always be found somewhere where you need a little more fun in combat.

After picking them up you have a really short time-window to throw it. Lack of movement in this duration is exchanged for heavy damage!
