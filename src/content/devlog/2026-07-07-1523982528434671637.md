- Reworked Nimue's boss mechanics: She no longer launches players upward when changing positions, preventing accidental flow interruptions and making hits feel fairer.

- Nimue's projectiles now apply Drift instead of a simple knock-up, allowing players to learn and recognize the mechanic used throughout the game.

- Nimue's movement behavior was improved. During her second phase, she automatically changes positions after a set time instead of only reacting to damage.

- Updated Nimue's water attack logic and warnings to better communicate the danger of entering the lake during her ultimate.

- Added Nimue's new Formless Mass: Flimmer.
 - Summons Nimue as a temporary ally.
 - Nimue attacks enemies and applies Drift.
 - Right-click activates Drown, allowing Nimue to execute a powerful beam attack against Drifted enemies.

- Drown can interact with any source of Drift, not only Nimue's own attacks, expanding the overall Drift system and allowing future abilities to combo with it.

- Added Nimue's new Boss Reward Chest with a full Tier 1-5 reward structure.

- Added exclusive Nimue rewards, including the Flimmer blueprint and Nimue's boss mask.

- Updated Nimue's reward system to use Sacred Relics for unique boss progression rewards.

- Nimue was already considered one of the stronger boss fights, so this update focuses on polishing, improving clarity, and expanding her mechanics rather than changing her identity.
