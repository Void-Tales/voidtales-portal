Remember her? One of my most beloved NPCs.

I have to admit that her quest stayed the same, but it feels 20 x better. With the new dialogue options and things you can answer she seems to have a much deeper character. It was something like: yo, get 3 mushrooms please.
Not you can experience how a skilled thief starts to believe in misfortune and slowly getting paranoid. When you helped her you will have access to even deeper conversation about the Mosslights and the Tempest Quiver.

Oh and when we are here already: Another gemstone which you can get when you were really nice to her has its own unique lore now as all of them will have soon.
