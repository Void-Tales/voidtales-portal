He claims that the Mistborn took his bones and sends you on a mission of hate. He wants you to slaughter them all. But this time it is different. He will regain memories when you bring him bones and you will notice soon enough that its not all his memories.

In the end the situation will get slightly out of hand and you will have to take a choice.
