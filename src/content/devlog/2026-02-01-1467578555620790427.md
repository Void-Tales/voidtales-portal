One of my greatest features in Void Tales is the Formless Mass.
It is an artefact of great potential and it can mimic skills and abilitys from enemys and bosses. The mechanic is not easy to overview and needs a little bit of understanding, but I reworked the way you will approach it.

Instead of the annoying notebook and flashy texts above Amelias head, you will have a little cutsceen now. After that Amelia is as surprised as you that here robot friend Chromo "zapped" you and you can talk with her.

For lore-enthusiasts there are some questions about her heritage and her friend of course, but a much better explanation how to use this feature is implemented, too.

As soon as you bring her a blueprint she is combining the void power with the copperwind technology, and Chromo decrypts 3 words of the void language and burns them into the Formless Mass.
