Vivalora, Fiore & Pei

Almost everybody who played Void Tales once crossed one of them. And they are always connected to other two of them. They are my biggest attempt to show a RPG side of the good old days and some of you even mentioned the feeling and remembrance to zelda and other games like this.

there is even an artwork from @Soph from Fiore!

I made a much better reattempt to this 3 OGs.

Fiore:

He is a little bit bigger then before and is always giving the player a nice way to think about this world. While doing so he is always challenging the gods and Phia with his words indirectly, but never directly. Even when this is what brought this three in their misery he will never stop to challenge the world, the gods, the people and of course every single height he can climb. So he remains the jump quest guy. Hate him or love him, there is no between.

Pei:

Pei stayed the same from the looks but he is more unique then ever. 
While he knows the world like his left wing and saw everything from above twice already you can notice that he struggles with his memories. He is remembering everything, just in the wrong order. He is traumatized by what has be done to them but tries to keep up his good mood. He will keep giving you advice and hints.

Vivalora:

She got the biggest change. From a harsh chicken treating you distant she became a warm hearted... chicken. No but she is obsessed with all the shiny and golden things people forgot or lost in the world. Before it was just materialism, but she has a severe trauma of loosing everything, thats why she is obsessed with finding things and even more: keeping them.
And her humor is not that bad anymore.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

They all have MUUUCH better dialogues now with different options you cna choose.
Pei & Vivalora will not talk with you and tread you like a stranger until you met Fiore at Owls Nest and climbed the tree.
Fiore can not be seen at their house until you met him at Owls Nest and climbed the tree.
Fiore will vanish at Owls Nest and appear at their house after you met him at Owls Nest and climbed the tree.
Vivaloras treasure quest has now a less confusing hint for the treasure and a completely other reward. A golden mask. I am implementing mask items which are just cosmetics basicaly but here and there some people are interested in them.
After you obtained the mask from the treaure quest you can talk to Vivalora again and see a completely other side of her.
This will lead to a difficult decision: Keep the mask or sell it to a really good prize.

I could talk all day about the changes I am doing in the last weeks. IT. IS. INCREDIBLE.
And this is only the first meeting with them.
They have great secrets and a long past.
