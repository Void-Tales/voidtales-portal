I started the quality update in Ibya Forest.

And this time I want to give you an example of how much better everything will be.

**Before:**
It was a linear dialogue attached to an NPC. The player essentially just listened while the character talked through anxious thoughts about the surrounding dangers. There was no interaction, no agency, and no change in outcome. The emotional idea was there: a nervous, overthinking character. But it stayed on the surface because the player wasn’t involved in it.

**Now:**
After the rework, the same core idea has been expanded into an actual experience.

The quest now has a clear setting, buildup, and escalation. Fintel isn’t just talking anymore. He’s placed in a physical situation, Fintel is found at the edge of the Void in Ibya Forest, slowly breaking down as he believes something is whispering to him and waiting to take him. The player can respond in different ways, but none of the choices change the outcome (Almost all other quests do now), but they change the emotional framing. The player can support him, dismiss him, provoke him, or reject him. Each response reveals a different side of Fintel’s state of mind.

Fintel’s mental state escalates until he finally gives the player a small box and asks them not to look back. As the player leaves, he disappears without explanation. Returning to the area shows no trace of him except his bag, containing strange items and hints tied to the Void like a short book about staring in the Void and of course some rewards.

The quest is now focused on atmosphere and uncertainty rather than simple dialogue, leaving the player unsure whether Fintel was imagining it or telling the truth. (If you ignore the loud scream and monster sound)
