The big book about every single gemstone in the overworld is here. It is an interactible interface where you can see which gemstones you have and which gemstones are already upgraded to the fabled version. If you never saw them at all they are just blacked out.

I tried to let them appear as some kind of star constellation and you can read in the book when you see it. But the thing is they belong to other people and it could be annoying for them when you always read in their books... but do not worry! You can buy your own copy for 8 gold and check them whenever you want.
