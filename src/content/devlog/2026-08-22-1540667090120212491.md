- The command “/parkour leave” now works again for everyone. It was listed in the sub-command list of the command whitelist, and this list blocks commands rather than allowing them.
- ‘/pk’ now works as a shorthand for ‘/parkour’. The whitelist checked the entered name and never resolved aliases.
- ‘/w’, ‘/tell’, ‘/reply’, ‘/r’, ‘/who’, ‘/pets’ and ‘/bal’ were no longer working for the same reason and are now working again.
- Modpack 2.1.1 has been released, with “ImmediatelyFast” updated to version 1.16.3 to fix the issue with missing letters and symbols that some of you had been experiencing.
- “OpenAudioMc” is running on a new build. Your audio settings are now saved again when you join and leave; since update 26.2, this had been failing silently.
- “BetonQuest” has been updated. Two entries for heirlooms in the “Gemcyclopadia”, which previously could not be loaded, are once again available in the menu.
