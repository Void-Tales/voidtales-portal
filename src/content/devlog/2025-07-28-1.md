Each armor piece now has its own bonus stat in addition to the set bonus stat.

The prices for the money pouches sold by Aurum Pridetail — which secure your gold on death — have been significantly reduced

All food items can now be consumed instantly

From now on, whenever you receive an item during an NPC dialogue, an extra line will appear to tell you what you just received

Fixed a bug that allowed the Rotbloom Blueprint to be obtained multiple times

The design of the Chrono Courier has been changed

Fixed a typo in the dialogue of the wolf who asks you to end his life

The number of Shelleys required to upgrade Loyalty has been increased

Cyn now has a greatly improved soundtrack – capturing the creeping madness, rising tension, and fitting for the "final boss" of Chapter 1

The purchase price for the dungeon key to the Hall Of Voices has been reduced from 200 to 60

There is now a "Progress Tree" for all armor sets, upgraded gear, shields, and weapons – perfect for collectors and those wanting to see what they might’ve missed

Shellshocks and Shellmites are working again – a bug was fixed that prevented them from triggering properly

The Fletchforge has been introduced — a specialized workbench where bow-type weapons can be freely swapped for crossbow-type weapons, and vice versa

The ranged weapon Sylvan Grace has now been added to the "Relics Of War" achievement tree.

A new system was introduced where players complete quests to unlock food that can be collected every 5 hours.
