Ok! A new area of the Soul Station is reworked with the new dialog system.
At first: the 2 Raccoons who are "guarding" the casino have their own new dialogue, and even if they are not really important you can go a little bit more in depth when talking with them.

And Aurum Pridetail, one of the wolves who never went insane with strict discipline (or greed).
You have to acknowledge this pride animal now and you can do that with 3 different ways.
I suggest you to kiss his paw, its just hilarious.

He will always talk different to you for the rest of your life, depending what you chose (and more).
And of course you can still open the shop to buy tokens as before.
And for the lore enthusiasts, he has some answers. But only if you ask of course!
