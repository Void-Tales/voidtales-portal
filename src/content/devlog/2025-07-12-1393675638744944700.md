5 unique bags have been created, each with character, story, and different purposes and sizes

Special gemstones have been introduced that boost certain stats at the cost of others (thanks @The Wandering Druid )

All upgrade levels of Loyalty have been integrated into the achievement system to provide an overview of acquired abilities

A mailbox system has been designed, allowing players to receive letters

Seagull Of Saltwind now marks all mailboxes, showing only seagulls handle mails in Void Tales

Various letters from different NPCs have been added, which players receive at specific story points

Noquun no longer recycles old armor, but upgrades it instead.

The boss "The Silken King" has been reworked with a custom soundtrack, 5 new attacks, 3 phases, and improved rewards

All spiders summoned by the Silken King now disappear when the boss dies or the player is out of range

All important spiders that are crucial for the bossfight now emit a glow

The "Watchers" of Umon now have a skill that traps players in webs, but for a much shorter duration than their usual Umon counterpart

Every boss now has an 8-minute respawn timer, visually indicated by a small snail crawling around the area

Whisperskin Parchment has been added as a slightly rarer resource drop from breakable vases

The Voidling is now a pet for early players

The third armor set has been created: the Lunargloom Set
