Alright!

I continued my quality-journey from Ibis Island, which has *a looooooot* of improvements now, to the **Grove Of Deities**.

Where should I begin?

- the old ways to interact with Phia and Ibis are gone
- both have much deeper ways of communication now
- some of Phias and Ibis dialogues are repeatable, some are not
- some of Phias and Ibis dialogues needs to be unlocked by doing certain things
- the audience item is now transfered to the grove of deities and is needed to awake Ibis
- Ibis has a whole new character model and ways to talk
- you will only understand the bloomari talking when you learned the language from one of them
- the skills of the given blade are now usable in the grove of deities
- phia will greet you here, but then only continue talking to you when you got her gift
- the given blade has its own "item-achieve" window as the weapons before now
- I added a unique sound for Ibis
- There is a better way of displaying the tooltips of the items now

It does not sound much, but it took me whole day to do this. I am trying to be as perfect as I can when it comes to Phia and Ibis.
