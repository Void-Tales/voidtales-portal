Maria Nelclair & Hunter Wilhelm
This one was a really fun rework because I finally feel like the hunters have a proper identity.

They represent an entire mindset. They are hunters, but not the kind who chase animals or simply protect nature. Their purpose is much darker: they hunt what has been touched and corrupted by Insanity.

I wanted this first meeting to feel like the beginning of something bigger. The player should not learn the whole story yet, but they should feel that there is a history behind these two. Small hints toward the Nelclair family, Faelan, and the corruption that will later consume them are introduced without revealing too much.

Maria is still doing her test with the bell!
One bow.
One arrow.
One chance.

I wrote a more complex dialogue, not only about her identity, she is giving you the bow now which breaks after 1 shot. And she will givbe it to you again, but only when it broke.

Wilhelm also received a small interaction where he tests the player's understanding of a hunter's mindset. Instead of freely giving his gemstone, the player has to prove that they understand the weapon they are about to use.

The correct answer rewards the player with a gemstone for the Offhand Crossbow. A wrong answer means the player will have to discover it later through Glimmer.

The biggest goal with this rework was to make the hunters feel like more than a tutorial for a new weapon.

Maria introduces the philosophy.
Wilhelm tests the understanding.
The Silent Steps test the skill.
