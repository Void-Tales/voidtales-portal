This was one of the most emotional rewrites I have worked on recently.

The Lady Of End started as a small tutorial NPC, a cat that asked the player a simple question before the world was shattered. Over time, I realized something important:

She is the only character who can prove that the tutorial actually happened.

The player spends so much of the story questioning what was real. Even Phia avoids giving direct answers about the past. The Shattering, Falwhind, the final moments before the world broke...

But the Lady Of End remembers.

She does not remember because of magic or prophecy. That is simply what she is. A strange, almost impossible creature!

The biggest change was making the old tutorial choice matter again.

When the player meets her hundreds of years later, she does not just say "welcome back". She remembers the exact answer the player gave her back then. The choice that seemed meaningless in the tutorial suddenly becomes proof that those small moments were always important.

This also gave us the opportunity to reveal something much bigger.

The Lady Of End remembers Princess Lyara.

The princess who gave the player their final mission before the Shattering. The person who sent them into the throne room, knowing they might never return.

Hearing her name again is not meant to be a simple lore drop. It is supposed to feel like a crack in the player's understanding of the world.

After that, the conversation slowly moves toward the Abyssal Tear.

Technically, this also became an interesting system change. The encounter now has different dialogue paths depending on whether the player spoke with her during the tutorial or walked past her. Both players eventually reach the same important revelation, but the journey feels different.
