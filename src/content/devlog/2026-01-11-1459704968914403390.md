Lady Merriweather!

I always liked her because she is a nice change from some of this darker beings at Soul Station.
Somehow even a normal person.
Her purpose before was to tell confused player that this airship is bringing them to Ibis Island, the start of your adventure!

Now she is basically doing the same, but she is talking about Emmeline, too.
Emmeline is the train of the Soul Station which brings the passenger to the place they desire.
So she will have new dialog unlocked as soon the player is summoning Emmeline for the first time.

And in addition you can ask her if Ibis Island is harboring one of the fragments, which will lead to an updated questjournal and more information about the first fragment: Ibis!
