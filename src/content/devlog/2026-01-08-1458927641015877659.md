This is the new Library-System.

Forget the old /story command where you open this weird lorebook-shop and forget unlocking lore with pages.
I think knowledge should be accessable as often as you want, as long you are one of these players who is interested in lore.

That means:
You talk with anyone who is telling you something important, or something significant about the world of Void Tales, it could happen that you wander around and this pile of books will tell you that you have a new book.
And the little symbol will even tell you which one is new.
