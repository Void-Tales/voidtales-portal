New Melodys Are Hearable Lately!

Alright guys, here comes something else.
As told yesterday the Blossomwind Flute is now the Flufftail Flute.

The Racoons and their origin homeland Tanuki are seen sometimes here & there and will play a major role some day in the future. So it comes that the flute that brings you back home has to be more important, too!

1. I added a much better custom designed sound from myself of a Shakuhachi flute into Void Tales. It replaced the boring noteblock sound of the minecraft flute.

2. You can now play the flute freely as you wish with 2 basic notes (left & right-click) and pitch them dependent of if you look up or down.

3. A completely new melody is added to unravel secrets, I show you an example here in the video. But it will be able to do much much more!

Bonus:
@BlissedYui had the idea once that every gemstone should have its own lore, and he is completely right. Absoloutely even! ❤️

So I started with the 2 gemstones you can get in the Gemadillo Questline, and now the first gemstone that was there already. I linked it to the Tanuki & Racoon lore and it is the reward for playing the melody at Soul Station here.
Oh and as I mentioned you can even unlock new dialogues with NPCs who are related to this place as soon as you have the flute : D

I hope I forgot nothing for now.
