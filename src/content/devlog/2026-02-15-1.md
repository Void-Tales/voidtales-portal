Oh wow! I did it! The first real quality questline of Void Tales.

You know there are plenty quests here but the dialogues are crazy long and they are linear.
**Not anymore!**

I wrote this quest 5 days long and its only the first. It has character, charme, decisions, plot twists,hints you can gather and 2 complete different outcomes which will decide which unique gemstone you will get. (You can read them before you decide)

Its not @BlissedYui approved yet, but it will for sure : D
You can find the questline already in-game and try it out.
Just talk to the Gemadillo Boss in their shop and see what can happen.

The real gem (no pun intended) of this quest is Mournis, a complete new NPC with some disturbing character traits which you will only find at night.

Have fun!

(so much work for 1 quest, but hey we try to be different. And better than the rest!)
