Some of you may remember the Shade Of Fading Day, a really weird name but I like it that much, yes : D

Back then she was upgrading your HP, which is not existent anymore, because every adventurer can play with HP builds now, depending on the gemstones you put in your armor and weapons.

She is giving blesings now, which are passive buffs you keep your entire storyline. But even this is not completely new.

I changed her personality from a weird ghostly figure, only talking cryptic stuff to an enjoying personality that takes care of the tree of her homeland Tanuki.
Tanuki is heavy connected with the Racoons lore-wise but thats another story.
She is enjoying the place so much that she offers you to take some rest whenever you visit here, which is a purely immersive feature that lets you sit on the bench and enjoying the cherry blossom tree for a while.

Edit: Now there are 2 Racoons joining the view as soon as you sit on the bench.
