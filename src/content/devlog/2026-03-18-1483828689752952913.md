I am really close to completing Ibis Island. 

It takes multiple days sometimes to rework 1 single quest, but I am satisfied in the end.
To be honest I am changing so much (to the better) that I cant even write it all here.

I try it:

Ashen Petals have a new design and "evolve" when you hit them now.
You dont have to do a jumpquest anymore to help Farmer Grace.
There is a new "quest" (dialogue-driven). The small Ellum does not feel so good, maybe you can help it.
The book lecterns telling the sentences of Phia are now gone and the text is written on the stone.

To Graces Quest:

You have to something completely different now. She has an Ellum which is much stronger and bigger than the others, but it is nervous and scared really fast. Your task is it to guide it to a much calmer space so Grace can shear it. The Ellum will follow you a few steps when you are interacting with it, so you have to be patient. The problem is, it hates the scarecrow.

After finishing this task, she will not pay you. But she will give you some knowledge where you can find wild Ellums and even tame one.
