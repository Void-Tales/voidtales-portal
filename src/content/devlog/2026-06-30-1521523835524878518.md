I changed the drop behavior from monsters. 
The red light on the ground which shows that there is an item is not just appearing anymore. I built a little "drop" animation with a sound and gravity so it "falls of" the mob. This is way more satisfying. There are now 3 different possible drops with their own chance, which is material, money or food. They were all together before in 1 drop.
