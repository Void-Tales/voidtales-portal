There was a trader, the practiced wanderer with his his little racoon friend. He was upgrading the Oak Sword to Witherwood.

Since Witherwood replaced the Oak Sword and the Moltanicas introduced a smooth upgrade system, I completely overhauled these 2.

They invite you to sit at their side at the campfire and talk a little bit. The racoon seems to collect some Shelleys lately and he wants to investigate it.

The quest has some more steps, different options that gives you different dialogues, a little funny rock throw sequence and takes a dive into the whole insanity & loyalty thematic.
