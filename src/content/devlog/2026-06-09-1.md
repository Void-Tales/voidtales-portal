Thanks to @snowflakekiwi the windmill has a new name!

She won the Logbranch Cliff event and the decision is falling on the botanic name of the kiwi, which is Actinidia (Genus, not Species). As a gardener I love this decision and as you guys know me I cant just rename the windmill without changing thr world around.

There is now a new buff food next to the cookies and berries, which is the Actinidia as well.
I added it to the casino rewards, ibya forest mob drops and around the windmill there are plenty of vases where you can just pick them into your inventory.

I like the fact that some people may revisit this place to refill their bags with this delicious fruits.
