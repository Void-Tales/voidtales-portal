I decided to continue today!
Does anyone remembers Agnes? She is taking your monster material for +1 gold. Thats all. 

I wanted to change that!
She has a much deeper dialog now with a "getting to know each other" phase. She will tell you that every monster or enemy can have a relentless will, and that this will can live on in their remains. It is your free will to tell her what do you think of that, but in the end she will be at your side again and offer you +1 gold for each mob material.

But this time you can ask her even more things about herself and why she is doing that. And even better:

If you sell monster material to her at night, it will give you +2 gold now, and even +3 gold on full moons.

Do you seek fast money or are you willing to sacrifice some place in your bags and wait to the night... or even longer : D

Oh and she will have a quest now!
