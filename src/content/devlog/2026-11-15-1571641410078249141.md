Maintenance · 07 is our first roguelike dungeon. You enter alone or with friends, pick one of the fallen heroes and their gear, and start from zero every single time. The rooms stay the same, but what waits inside, what you find and how you build your run changes with every attempt.

Deep below Ibis Island lies an old Copperwind facility that has kept itself running for far too long. It is loud, it is busy, and it is far too happy to see you. It should feel like stepping into a place that never stopped working, even after everyone else did.

What you see here are only the first two rooms, a small part of the whole dungeon. The boss music is not in yet.

What is coming down there will be unbelievable.
