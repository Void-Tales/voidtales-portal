A complete new system with HUD integration called momentum was implemented

When arriving at Ibya Grove, you can now hear Cyn howling in the distance once. There are new optional dialogues about this howling with Maria and Lady Of End.

There is now a hidden dialogue in the Moonlit Spire to aquire a key. This key can open a chest at the very top of the spire. To get their you can use one of the experiments the arcanists are doing.

There are now new skills for weapons, new bufffood and new gemstones, all are based on the momentum mechanic

The new gemstones have been added to the Gemcyclopadia, the Glimmer rewards and the reward chest at the end of the Hall Of Voices
