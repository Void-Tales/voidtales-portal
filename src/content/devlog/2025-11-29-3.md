A brand new training area called Somewhere has been created.

New custom music has been added to Somewhere. 

Somewhere is accessible to every player through a book purchased from the vending machine. 

New training weapons have been introduced. 

New training gems are available specifically for use in Somewhere. 

New dummies with unique abilities have been added. 

A new way to display damage and combos has been implemented on certain training dummies. 

The new Combo Dummy has been added to both Soul Station and Falwhind (the old one still exists). 

The Hunter Bell, used for bow training, now only shows its “Shard Bell” message in Ibya Forest and nowhere else. 

A new (fun) Straw Dummy Boss has been added to Somewhere. 

A new achievement has been created for defeating the Straw Dummy Boss.
