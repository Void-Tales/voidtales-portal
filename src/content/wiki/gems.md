Gemstones socket into your weapons and armor and shape how your build plays - some empower
critical strikes, others reduce cooldowns or enhance abilities directly.

## Gemstone types

- **Rift, Hollow, Vessel** - the base gemstone types
- **Bleak** - rare gemstones that strengthen the cursed blade Loyalty
- **Heirloom** - grant power at the cost of something else
- **Fragment** - seven of these exist, one for each of Phia's fragments, the ancient deities.
  Ibis, the first fragment, has already been awakened
- **Moltanica** - instead of being consumed, a Moltanica upgrades the item it's socketed into
  directly and permanently

## Current rules

Every weapon and armor piece has **one** gemstone slot, and that slot accepts any gemstone
type. Items progress through Base, Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 - some unlock a second gemstone
slot at their final tier.

## Stats gemstones affect

Tempo, Crit Chance / Crit Damage, Damage Reduction, Dodge Chance, Health, Recovery, Lifesteal
Chance / Power, and Parry Chance / Power / Counter.

Every player has a small baseline chance (~2%) to trigger Crits, Lifesteal, or Parries even
without gear, so builds aren't strictly gated by specific gemstones.

Want the details on any individual stat? The **Stat Library**, an interactive book in the
library corner, explains all of them in-game.

## Still evolving
