Void Tales is a story server, not a server built around `/home` and `/tpa` - the command list
below is short on purpose.

## Commands

- `/guide` (aliases `/journalbook`, `/guidebook`) - opens the Journal, your active quest log
- `/stuck` - frees you if you get physically stuck in a block or the terrain
- `/pets` - pet management, with subcommands `spawn`, `mount`, `inventory`, `name`, `open`,
  `category`, `petFood`, `item`, `signalstick`, `clearStats`
- `/parkour`, `/parkour leave` - join and leave parkour courses
- `/titles` - manage your player title
- `/audio` - connect to the server's browser-based audio
- `/money`, `/balance`, `/baltop` - the server economy
- `/msg <player>`, `/r` - private messages and reply
- `/list`, `/help` - see who's online, get command help

## Features

- **Live world map:** [dynmap.voidtales.win](https://dynmap.voidtales.win)
- **Custom HUD** showing your stats and progress
- **Quests and Journal** tracked through `/guide`
- **Pets** you can raise, mount, and equip
- **Parkour courses** scattered around the world
- **Gemstone-powered builds** - see [Gems](/wiki/gems) for the full system
