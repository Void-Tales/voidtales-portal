Void Tales is a story server, not a server built around `/home` and `/tpa` - the command list
below is short on purpose.

## Commands

- `/guide` (aliases `/journalbook`, `/guidebook`) - opens the Journal, your active quest log
- `/stuck` - frees you if you get physically stuck in a block or the terrain
- `/pets` (alias `/mcpets`) - opens the pet menu, where you spawn, mount, rename and equip
  your pets
- `/parkour` (alias `/pk`), `/parkour leave` - join and leave parkour courses
- `/titles` - manage your player title
- `/audio` (aliases `/media`, `/sound`, `/vc`) - connect to the server's browser-based audio
- `/money`, `/balance` (alias `/bal`), `/baltop` - the server economy
- `/msg <player>` (aliases `/w`, `/tell`, `/pm`), `/r` (alias `/reply`) - private messages
- `/list` (alias `/who`), `/help` - see who's online, get command help

## Features

- **Live world map:** [dynmap.voidtales.win](https://dynmap.voidtales.win) - 3D since the 26.2
  update. The link did not change
- **Custom HUD** showing your stats and progress
- **Quests and Journal** tracked through `/guide`
- **Pets** you can raise, mount, and equip
- **Parkour courses** scattered around the world
- **Gemstone-powered builds** - see [Gems](/wiki/gems) for the full system

## In Discord

Our Discord bot answers a few things without you being in-game: `/mcstatus` for the server
status, `/playerlist` for who is online, `/playerstats` and `/leaderboard` for your numbers,
`/achievement` for achievements, and `/gallery` to submit a screenshot.
