The main way to reach the team is the ticket system in our Discord.

## Opening a ticket

Run `/ticket` (or the legacy `!ticket`) and pick a category from the dropdown:
**Application** 📝, **Bug** 🐛, or **Support** 🛠️. Add a short description if you have one, and a
private channel opens with just you and the team in it.

Once a ticket is open, a team member can claim it, a status button shows where things stand,
and closing/reopening is available to you as the ticket creator too. When a ticket closes, a
transcript is generated automatically.

## What to include in a bug report

- Your in-game name
- What happened
- Where it happened (region or coordinates)
- Roughly when it happened
- A screenshot, if you have one

## Other ways to reach us

- `/suggest <text>` in Discord starts a 7-day poll in the suggestions channel
- `/preferences` toggles a weekly summary on or off
- `/help` lists the bot's commands
- In-game, `/stuck` gets you out if you're physically stuck
