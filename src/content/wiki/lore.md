Void Tales has been in development for over 13 years, and the lore has grown with it. It's not
shoved in your face - you find it through NPCs, item descriptions, and environmental
storytelling as you explore.

## What's confirmed so far

- **Insanity** is an ever-present influence across the world
- A tragic conflict runs between **Phia** and **Faelan**
- **Loyalty**, a cursed blade, plays a central role
- Phia has **seven fragments** - the ancient deities themselves. **Ibis**, the first, has
  already been awakened

Chapter 1 is playable now; Chapter 2 is in development.

## Want more?

The lore video series on YouTube goes deeper into the world's background, and [/news](/news)
covers new lore as it's revealed. This page stays a high-level overview on purpose - the rest
is worth discovering in-game.
